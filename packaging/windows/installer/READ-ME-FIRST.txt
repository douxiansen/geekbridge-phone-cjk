GeekBridge Phone for Windows
============================

Calls, texts and contacts from your phone on your computer, with the call voice through the
computer's speakers and microphone. No phone app, no account, no cloud.

Needs: Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11, a Bluetooth adapter, an Android phone
or an iPhone.

Install
-------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere.
2. Right-click Install.ps1 and choose "Run with PowerShell". Windows asks for administrator
   rights once. The script trusts the GeekBridge certificate (GeekBridge.cer) and installs the
   package (GeekBridge.Phone.msix). Nothing else changes.
3. Start "GeekBridge Phone" from the Start menu.

Why a certificate? Windows only lets an installed app take phone calls, and this package is
signed by the project itself rather than a paid certificate authority. You can inspect
GeekBridge.cer before trusting it. Uninstall.ps1 removes the app and, if you say yes, the trust.

First use
---------
1. In the app, pair your phone (the app finds it and shows a number to compare).
2. On the phone: Settings > Bluetooth > the gear next to this computer > switch on "Calls"
   (call audio). If it switches itself off, unpair the phone in the app and pair once more.
3. Ring the phone. A banner appears at the top right; press Answer and talk through the computer.
